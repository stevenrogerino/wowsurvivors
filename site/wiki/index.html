Chapter One

The vigil

Every night, the dark comes up out of the ground.

It has taken every watch before this one.

What holds it back is ember, and ember will not burn on its own.

It sleeps in the stones the dead leave behind.

Break the stone. Take the light out of it. While you carry it, you burn.

They can smell a light from a long way off.

You will not kill your way out of this. Nobody ever has.

You will out-burn it, and you will keep moving.

Hold until the light comes back.

Your weapons fire themselves

Everything you carry swings, casts and reloads on its own. Your whole job is where you stand.

Walk over the gems

Level up and choose: a new weapon, a rank on one you carry, or a passive.

Six weapons, and no more

Rank one to 8 and learn its paired passive and it evolves. Two evolved weapons can become one, and hand the slot back.

Thirty minutes, and then dawn

Bosses arrive on a schedule and the horde thickens. At 30:00 the field is purged, you breathe, and whatever has been behind all of it comes out to meet you.

Gold outlives the run

Keep every coin whether you win, die, or walk away, and spend it with the Trainer.

What the ember does to you

Every survivor climbs the same six-tier ladder as they rank up in a run: the hem takes light, the shoulders build, the cloak lengthens into sparks, a crown of embers, burning pinions, and at last the brand, the ember showing through the chest.

Passives

Might 5 ranks

+10% weapon damage

Multiplies the damage of every weapon you carry.

Haste 5 ranks

-8% weapon cooldowns

Every weapon fires more often. Ranks stack multiplicatively.

Fleetfoot 5 ranks

+12% movement speed

You move faster.

Greed's Pull 5 ranks

+25 pickup radius

Gems, coins, and potions fly to you from farther away.

Vitality 5 ranks

+25 maximum health (and heals it)

A bigger health pool, plus a free heal when taken.

Ironhide 5 ranks

+2 armor (diminishing damage reduction)

Every hit is cut by a share based on your armor. Each point helps a little less than the last, and it never blocks a hit completely.

Precision 5 ranks

+7% critical strike chance

Chance for any weapon hit to strike critically.

Ferocity 4 ranks

+25% critical strike damage

Your critical strikes hit even harder.

Expanse 5 ranks

+12% effect area

Bigger auras, zones, orbits and novas. Chain and beam weapons gain REACH from this (longer leaps, longer lines), not damage. Look to pierce or count for those instead.

Duplicity 3 ranks

+1 projectile for volley weapons

Adds a projectile to bolts, knives, arrows, axes, shields, storm strikes, chain leaps, palms, beasts and rings. Auras and beams are unaffected.

Fortune 4 ranks

+15% luck (better and more drops)

Better odds of coins, potions, sapper charges, and lodestones, but every rank here is a rank not spent on clear speed, which is what actually keeps a build alive early. A strong late-game luxury, a weak early priority.

Wisdom 4 ranks

+10% experience gained

Every gem is worth more experience.

Recovery 4 ranks

+0.5 health regenerated per second

Steady healing, always ticking.

Velocity 3 ranks

+15% projectile speed

Bolts, knives, arrows, and shields fly faster. No effect on chains, auras, gyres, or storms.

Dark Bargain 3 ranks

The horde grows: +20% spawns, +8% enemy speed. You grow too: +15% XP and gold.

A curse you choose. More enemies means more gems, more gold, more danger. Stacks.

Warding Light 3 ranks

Blocks the next single hit against you completely, then recharges.

Blocks one hit every 30 / 22 / 15 seconds by rank.

Chilling Presence 3 ranks

Enemies near you are slowed by cold.

Slows non-boss enemies within a widening frost aura (stronger and wider per rank). The aura grows with effect area.

Spirit Companion 3 ranks

Summon a spirit wolf that hunts the horde.

Each rank calls another spirit wolf. They chase down the nearest enemies and maul them. Bites deal area damage that scales with your damage and level.

Thorns 5 ranks

Attackers take damage back, melee and ranged.

Any enemy that hits you, with a melee swing OR a ranged bolt, takes 10 + 40% of that damage back per rank (even if you dodge or block). Ranged bolts reflect to the caster that fired them.

Searing Aura 4 ranks

A holy aura sears nearby enemies.

Burns enemies around you twice per second (damage and radius grow with rank, damage%, and effect area). Lets tanks deal damage while standing firm.

Evasion 4 ranks

+8% chance to avoid a hit entirely.

Each rank adds a chance to completely dodge an incoming hit. Stacks with armor and blocks.

Curdled Light 5 ranks

+15% of your healing lashes out as shadow damage

Healing you cannot use rots instead of going to waste: overheal erupts around you, and a growing share of the healing that does land strikes with it. Feeds on regeneration, lifesteal, and healing weapons.

Ruin Hunger 5 ranks

+25% fel from overkill, +1s of Ruinform, and a shorter wait before it can answer again

Every scrap of overkill feeds the ruin faster, the transformation holds a second longer per rank, and the recovery window after it ends shrinks too, though it never fully closes unless the ruin is already part of you.

Grave Call 3 ranks

Raise a ghoul to shamble after the horde.

Each rank raises another ghoul. Slower than a spirit wolf and slower to swing, but it hits far harder and its claws sweep a wider arc.

Dread Command 5 ranks

+30% summon damage and +10% summon attack speed

Drives everything you have summoned, spirit wolves and ghouls alike, to strike harder and more often. Worthless without something to command.

Primal Kinship 5 ranks

+10% Wild from every kill, +1.5s in a shape, +6% damage while you wear one, and the Wild wakes sooner

The shapes come quicker, hold longer and hit harder, and the wait after one ends shrinks by 0.75s a rank, down to 3s.

Serenity 5 ranks

Steps return 0.5s sooner and palm 15% harder; a third step at rank 3 and a fourth at rank 5

Stillwater Step, deepened: every rank shortens the wait for a step (never below 2.5s) and strengthens the palm that lands along it.

Serration 4 ranks

Critical strikes open a wound that bleeds 30% of the blow per rank

The bleed runs over 3s and ticks every 0.5s. A fresh crit reopens the wound at whichever bleed is worse, so it rewards crit chance and big hits alike.

Perennial 5 ranks

+10% duration on everything that lingers

Zones, gyres, beasts and anything else with a lifetime stays on the field longer. It does not change how often a weapon fires.
