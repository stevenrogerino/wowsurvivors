The Ember Watch · Balance patch

The sun also rises.

Bosses that would not die. Survivors who could not live. Aura builds that flattened the horde and then spent twelve minutes chipping at one machine. We sent 3,897 bot games through every survivor, battlefield and difficulty to find out why, then fixed it.

The stalemate

Nobody sits there forever now.

A tester sent a video: a finale boss that could not kill the survivor, and a survivor who could not kill the boss. Twelve minutes of nothing. Now the dawn takes a side.

New

The sun is rising

After 3:00 of finale fight, the boss and everything bolted to it take +50% damage for every minute that passes. A toast tells you when it starts. A build that opens the gates quickly never sees it.

On Veteran, stalemates fell from 15% to 1% of finales. On Professional Hyper, from 42% to none.

Finale health

The fight measures the right thing.

Finale bosses grow tougher for strong builds ("It has taken your measure"). They used to measure all your damage, so a build that cleared screens of fodder brought a boss with up to 20× the health and no way to kill it. Now they measure what your kit does to one target, capped at ×6.

Easier

Harder settings, not longer fights

Difficulty and Hyper now raise finale health by the square root of their product. Their damage still counts in full, so Professional Hyper is deadlier, not a sponge.

Fix

The Pale Lord stops sulking at 40%

A rounding error meant about one health total in sixteen left him stuck on his winter line forever. 8 of 219 test finales hit it. Same guard added to four other phase lines.

Kept

Professional Hyper still bites

About 45% of Professional Hyper finale attempts still end in death. That is the hardest setting in the game working as intended.

Weapons vs bosses

Aura builds finally hit bosses hard enough.

Measured on real bosses, the best and worst weapons were 17× apart. Area weapons that shred a crowd barely scratched a single big target. Seventeen weapons now deal extra damage to bosses, elites and finale machines, shown in the tooltip as Vs bosses ×2.9.

The first minute

The hardest maps stop ending at 0:40.

On Professional Hyper, the Pale Wastes and Highmoor killed almost everyone in the first minute. Difficulty now eases in: 35% of the extra toughness at 0:00, all of it by 6:00. The top early killers also got a fairer tell.

New

Fell in the finale? Try again.

New

Retry the finale as often as you like

The dawn is banked the moment you reach 30:00, so dying to the finale boss no longer ends the run. You can Try again: back on your feet at full health with the kit you fell with, a six-second breath, and the fight from the top.

No second blessing and no Beans. A win on a retry still plays the ending, but it does not count toward the finale record or its achievement.

Asked and answered

Is Ruin Unbound broken?

Checked, not changed

It tops the real-game boss chart because of where it lives, not what it is. The builds that forge it stack 40% crit, 2.5× damage and the Ruinous Pact, and its homing bolts never miss while you dodge. Give every weapon the same kit on a training dummy and Ruin Unbound lands in the middle:

Axe Gyre3,321
Umbral Bolt2,831
Cinderfall2,193
Stormcall1,861
Ruin Unbound1,788
Firmament1,739

Damage per second on one boss, same late-game kit for every weapon, 30 seconds each. Ruin Unbound is weaker on a single target than either of the two weapons it is forged from. Builds that carry it kill late bosses in a median 27 to 34 seconds; the field runs from 20 to 41.

Small things

Also in this patch

Fix

Gems stay on the field

Kills right at the edge dropped experience you could not reach. Gems now land inside the field.

New

"Vs bosses" in the tooltip

Weapons with a boss factor show it with the rest of their stats, so you can plan a boss-killing slot.

Fix

Finale hits trimmed

The three hits that took the most health per minute in their fights: Kael's storm, the Heart-Drill's Frost Nova, and the Admiral's dash and powder keg.

How we tested

3,897 simulated games, run by a bot that dodges telegraphs and plays each survivor's build: every survivor, all six battlefields, Beginner to Professional Hyper, full 30-minute runs, finale replays from the same dawn kits, and the Eclipse Arena. Figures on this page compare the game before and after this patch on matching runs. The survivor health and arena difficulty changes are new in this patch and were not part of those runs.
