The Ember Watch · Balance patch

Even ground.

A tester's strongest builds could stand still from 20:00 and win, and a handful of abilities did most of every build's damage: Ruin Unbound half the meter, Conviction most of Keegan's, Grave Tether most of everyone's healing. We measured every weapon, union, discovery and calling against the same crowd and the same boss, then brought them into one band.

Asked again, answered this time

Ruin Unbound: half the meter to a fifth.

Last patch we checked it on a training dummy and left it alone. Testers kept seeing it at half the damage meter, and they were right: its homing, piercing, splashing bolts reach the crowd first and take every kill.

Nerf
Before this patch59%
Now16%

Its share of a build's damage, next to the same four evolved weapons, at 21:00 on the Pale Wastes, Professional, Hyper. Every union now sits between 15% and 30%.

How it was nerfed

  1. A smaller burst. Each bolt's explosion shrank from 82 to 55 wide, less than half the ground it covered (45%). It catches far fewer creatures with every bolt.
  2. Less damage over a fight. With the same four weapons beside it, it deals 12.0k a second, down from 17.9k: a third less.
  3. No longer takes every kill. With a smaller burst it stops reaching the whole crowd first, so its slice of the meter fell from 59% to 16% and your other weapons get the rest.

Each bolt now shows 37.9 damage, up from 25.5, because every evolved weapon was re-scaled in this patch and Ruin Unbound's bolts were raised along with the weapons it is made from. The smaller burst more than cancels that: each bolt hits harder, but it hits far fewer creatures.

Weapons, rank 1 to evolved

Every weapon, one band, all night.

Every weapon was measured at five points of a night (rank 1 at 1:00, rank 3 at 5:00, rank 5 at 9:00, rank 8 at 14:00, evolved at 20:00), with that point's share of passives, against the crowd a kiting survivor really faces then, a boss, and the real waves. Area weapons did two to five times anything else before they evolved; chains, bounces and bolts barely cleared a crowd until they did. Now each stays in its role's band the whole way. "At 5:00" is damage per second that actually lands on creatures in the real waves, at rank 3. "Evolved" is rank 8 and evolved, every passive capped, at 20:00 on the Pale Wastes, Professional, Hyper, against 320 targets standing where a real horde stands and one boss that closes and gets kited off.

Unions

Every union takes a fair share.

Unions were measured the way testers read them: the share of a build's damage each one takes beside four evolved weapons. New: the callings

Eight survivors get a power of their own.

The four newest survivors each carry a whole system of their own. The first eight now do too: a legendary blessing anyone can take, always offered to its owner, who gets one small edge (exactly a quarter on one number).

New

Spellflood

Baron Zul · Mage

Gems swell the Flood. Full, every weapon fires and cooldowns run at half for 5 seconds.

New

Radiant Barrier

Chid · Priest

Overheal becomes a barrier that takes hits first and bursts as Light when it breaks. It stops refilling for 2s after a hit and 6s after it breaks, so a crowd can wear it down.

New

Crits build Edge. Full, a Cutthroat blow on the toughest thing near you, then you slip from sight.

New

New

New

Ember, spring and gale stones rise where you stand. The ember stone's burn grows 0.4 a level, not 1.2: on its own it outdid every weapon late (282k a second to a crowd, now 104k).

New

Conviction

Professor Keegan · Paladin

Blows you take and healing you receive build it. At 36, Judgement: every creature on the field is struck down and bosses lose 4% of their health, with a 2s Aegis. Then 20s before it gathers again.

Reworked

Conviction was a Hammerfall

It filled at 3, rested 4 seconds, and dropped a blast around Keegan every few seconds. It was 60% of a tester's damage meter and 48% of his damage standing still, and its Aegis kept him untouchable nearly half the time. Judgement is rare and total: one or two in ten minutes of standing still, more when you take the fight to them. It is now under 7% of his damage.

Fix

The Aegis lasts as long as it shows

The hit that filled Conviction raised the gold dome, then its own half-second grace cut the protection short. Blows got through while the dome was still up. Now it holds for the full two seconds.

Small things

Also in this patch

Fix

Highmoor's storms

A setting added for Conviction overwrote Highmoor's own, so every storm strike landed at 190 wide instead of 88, nearly five times the area. Back to 88.

Buff

Axe Gyre in a crowded build

The meter was right: ranged weapons killed what would have reached the blades. Blades now deal 65% of the weapon's damage per touch, not 50% (Stormcall too).

Fix

Haste and Duplicity reach Seeking Motes

A volley of motes (and Iron Palms' flurry) leaves one shot at a time, and the next cast used to cut the last one off before it finished. Evolved, more motes or a faster cast added nothing (×0.95 and ×1.00). The shots now fit inside the cooldown: ×1.5 and ×1.4.

Fix

Velocity no longer throws seeking bolts wide

A faster seeking bolt turned in a wider circle and swung past what it was chasing: Velocity at its cap cut an evolved Seeking Motes to 60%. Seeking bolts now turn faster as they fly faster.

Fix

Six weapons means six

The Graveblade and twin glaives could hand over a seventh or eighth weapon. The cap now holds everywhere.

Fix

Overtime after a finale

The horde after a finale no longer counts the finale's length, and Death's first visit comes a full interval after overtime begins.

Fix

Charge lanes stay put

A charge's warning lane stays where the charge began instead of riding along with the creature.

New

Readable meters

Every weapon keeps a row on the damage meter, named for what did it, and healing from a weapon is credited to that weapon.

What to expect

Ordinary builds last longer late.

Raising the weakest weapons did more than trimming the strongest. The average weapon does what it did before, but a plain six-weapon build that used to fall 13 seconds after 20:00 now holds out for about four and a half minutes. The tester's strongest builds still hold out to the end; the damage behind them is just spread across more of their weapons.

How we tested

Each weapon, union, discovery and calling was measured alone, evolved at rank 8, at 20:00 on the Pale Wastes, Professional, Hyper: against 320 targets placed where a real 20:00 horde was measured standing, against one boss that closes in and gets kited off, and in the real waves. Unions were measured by their share of a build's damage with four other evolved weapons. The tester's builds were run standing still from 20:00. Blessings were measured on every survivor, thirty runs each. The previous patch notes are here.
